Training fits the fallback, candidates, and heads. Selection chooses one proposal; acceptance evaluates its teacher-relative harm bound and response consistency. Test data do not select candidates or change decisions. The task-batch setting differs from online single-example and task-agnostic class-incremental learning. Witness examples remain retained historical data and count toward persistent bytes.

\section{Conditional Guarantees}
For teacher $f$ and candidate $g$ fixed before acceptance, define $H=\ind\{f(x)=y,\ g(x)\ne y\}$. Pointwise,
\[
\ind\{g(x)\ne y\}-\ind\{f(x)\ne y\}\le H,
\]
so $\risk(g)-\risk(f)\le\E H$. An IID binomial upper bound controls this expectation with coverage $1-\alpha_t$ \cite{clopper1934}. Class-stratified sampling requires independent within-class samples and fixed mixture weights, permitting a classwise union bound and weighted aggregation. A predeclared sequence with $\sum_t\alpha_t\le\alpha$ controls coverage failures across sequential admissions if each proposal is fixed before its fresh acceptance observations.

The statement is that the probability of any false certified admission is at most $\alpha$, under these assumptions. It is not a posterior failure probability conditional on admission, absolute-risk guarantee, proof of semantic abstraction, or protection against shift. Additional screens can reduce admissions without establishing absolute competence. In the image experiments there are no accepted routes, so their accepted-route guarantee is vacuous.

The budget invariant assumes the initial non-evictable state fits and every publication is byte checked. It does not bound peak construction memory. Persistence requires immutability of the complete route, including preprocessing and buffers. An immutable inaccurate function stays inaccurate.

\section{Unestablished Claims and Artifact Limits}
The record does not establish a positive accuracy--memory frontier, lower end-to-end compute, a cross-task sharing benefit, semantic abstractions, a response-gate ablation benefit, or successful image substitution. No unseen-order robustness, long-stream scaling, shift robustness, latency distribution, peak workspace, or energy result is inferred. Public modular and compressed-replay methods remain contextual citations rather than completed reproductions \cite{mendez2021,veniat2021,hayes2020,pellegrini2019,nori2025}.

The repository contains manuscript sources, bibliography, deterministic schematic sources and assets, not the experimental implementation, notebook attachments, raw campaign artifacts, or data. The table registry maps results to evidence and limitations. Independent audit reproduction would require configurations, splits, source/checkpoint hashes, decision traces, accuracy matrices, byte ledgers, and environment records. This is a release limitation, not a new experimental plan. The appendix is compiled and uploaded separately; neither manuscript includes an identifying repository URL.
